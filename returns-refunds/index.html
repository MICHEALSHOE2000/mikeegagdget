Customer satisfaction is our priority.

Overview

At Mikee Gadget Plug, customer satisfaction is our priority. Because we sell electronic devices, we give refunds only for items with a verified factory defect, or with a fault that was present when you received the item. We do not give refunds for problems caused by misuse or damage after delivery. This policy covers orders placed on www.mikeegadget.com.ng and purchases at our store in Computer Village, Ikeja.

Eligibility for refunds

A refund or replacement request is valid if:

  1. the product has a manufacturer or factory defect, or (for UK Used devices) a fault that was present when you received it, or you received the wrong item;
  2. you make the request within 7 days of delivery or collection; and
  3. you return the product in its original packaging with all accessories and your proof of purchase (order reference or receipt), signed out of all accounts (Apple ID / iCloud, Google, Samsung).

Non-refundable cases

We will not give a refund if:

  • the item was damaged through negligence, misuse, a drop, a power surge or water damage;
  • the product seal or packaging has been tampered with;
  • the problem comes from improper installation, software modification or repair by anyone other than Mikee Gadget Plug;
  • the item was bought in a Hot Deal, clearance or promo sale, unless it is defective; or
  • you changed your mind after delivery or collection. Please check the model, storage, colour, condition and network notes (for example eSIM only or Wi‑Fi Only) before you pay.

Verification process

Our technical team inspects every returned product. If we confirm a factory defect, or a fault that was present at delivery, you can choose:

  • a full refund, processed within 5–10 business days; or
  • a replacement of the same model, subject to availability.

If the product passes inspection and works as it should, we return it to you and no refund is given.

Return procedure

  1. Within 7 days of receiving your item, contact support@mikeegadget.com.ng or WhatsApp 0708 686 5133.
  2. Give your order number, photos or videos of the defect, and a short description of the problem.
  3. Once your request is approved, we send instructions to drop the product at our Computer Village store or hand it to our logistics partner.

Shipping costs

  • If the return is due to a factory defect, a fault present at delivery, or our mistake, Mikee Gadget Plug pays the return shipping cost.
  • If a product is returned without a valid reason, you pay the shipping cost.

Refund timeline

Approved refunds go to your original payment method within 5–10 business days after inspection and confirmation:

  • Card or bank transfer (Paystack): refunded to the same card or account through Paystack.
  • Paid in store: refunded by bank transfer to an account in the buyer’s name.
  • Pay Small Small (Credit Direct): we notify Credit Direct, and the loan is cancelled or settled under Credit Direct’s terms. Any down payment you made is refunded to you.

Cancellations and payment problems

  • You can cancel an order before it is dispatched or collected and receive a full refund.
  • If we cannot supply an item you have paid for, you can choose an alternative or a full refund.
  • An order is placed only after your payment is confirmed. If you were charged for a failed payment, or charged twice, contact us with the payment reference and we will refund the extra amount in full.

Nothing on this page limits any right you have under Nigerian consumer protection law.
