Refund and Returns Policy

  • Delivery

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains when you can return a product or get a refund, and how to ask for one. It applies to orders placed on this website and to purchases at our store in Computer Village, Ikeja.

1. When you can get a refund, repair or replacement

Contact us within 7 days of receiving or collecting your product if:

  • the product is faulty;
  • you received the wrong product, storage size, colour or condition; or
  • the product is materially different from its description on our website.

After we check the product, we will repair it or replace it with the same item. If we cannot do either, we will give you a full refund.

2. Change of mind

We do not accept returns for change of mind once a product has been delivered to you or collected. Please check the model, storage, colour, condition (Brand New or UK Used), network notes (for example eSIM only or Wi‑Fi Only) and the total before you pay. If you are unsure, ask us first on WhatsApp.

3. Cancelling an order

  • Before dispatch or collection: you can cancel and receive a full refund.
  • If we cannot supply your order (for example, the item is no longer in stock), we will offer you an alternative or a full refund. The choice is yours.
  • Pricing errors: if a product was clearly listed at the wrong price, we will contact you before dispatch. You can pay the correct price or cancel for a full refund.

4. Condition of returned products

To qualify, a returned product must:

  • come back with its box, charger and any accessories supplied with it;
  • be free of physical damage, liquid damage or tampering that happened after delivery;
  • not have been opened or repaired by anyone other than Mikee Gadget Plug; and
  • be signed out of all accounts (Apple ID / iCloud, Google, Samsung), with screen locks removed.

The 7-day warranty does not cover damage caused by accidents, misuse, liquid or unauthorised repairs.

5. How to request a refund or return

  1. Contact us on WhatsApp or call 0708 686 5133 within 7 days. Give your order reference, describe the problem and, if you can, send photos or a short video.
  2. We reply within 2 business days and tell you how to return the product. You can bring it to our store or we will arrange collection. If the product is faulty or we sent the wrong item, we pay the return delivery cost.
  3. We inspect the product, usually within 2 business days of receiving it, and tell you the outcome.

6. How refunds are paid

  • Paid by card or bank transfer (Paystack): we refund the same payment method through Paystack. We start the refund within 3 business days of approving it. It usually reaches your account within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.
  • Paid in store: refunded by bank transfer to an account in the buyer’s name.
  • Pay Small Small (Credit Direct): we tell Credit Direct about the return, and the loan is cancelled or settled under Credit Direct’s terms. Any down payment you made is refunded to you.

The refund is the amount you paid for the product, including any delivery charge. If a payment failed but you were still charged, or you were charged twice, contact us with the payment reference and we will refund the extra amount in full.

7. Failed or cancelled payments

An order is placed only after the payment provider confirms your payment. If a payment fails or is cancelled, no order is created. Any amount that was held is released by your bank.

Nothing on this page limits any right you have under Nigerian consumer protection law.

Questions? Chat on WhatsApp or call 0708 686 5133. Store: 1 Ola Ayeni Street, Off Simbiat Abiola Way, Computer Village, Ikeja, Lagos.
