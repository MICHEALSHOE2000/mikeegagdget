Brand new vs UK used: which should you buy?

Brand new phones are sealed, never used and usually the newest models. UK used phones are fairly used phones imported from the UK. They cost much less for the same model, which is why most buyers in Lagos choose them for older flagships like the iPhone 14 Pro Max, 15 Pro Max or Galaxy S23 Ultra.

When buying UK used, the things that matter are battery health, Face ID (on iPhones), the screen and the camera. Read our iPhone battery health guide and UK used iPhone checklist before you pay anyone.

What does "WiFi-only" mean?

A WiFi-only iPhone is locked to a foreign network, so calls, mobile data and SIM or eSIM service may not work in Nigeria. It works on WiFi for apps, internet, camera and everything else. People buy them as a cheaper second phone, for content creation, or for use at home and in the office. Never buy one as your main phone unless you're sure you don't need a SIM.

Why phone prices change in Nigeria

  • The exchange rate. Almost every phone is imported and priced in dollars, so a weaker naira means higher prices within days.
  • New launches. When Apple, Samsung or Google release a new model, the previous model usually drops in price.
  • Supply. Popular colours and storage sizes sell out first and can cost more.
