What a Web Developer Needs: Laptop, Test Phones, Tablet & Setup (2026)

By Mikee Gadget Plug • Updated November 26, 2026 • 6 min read

Short answer

A web developer needs a laptop with 16GB RAM and an SSD, at least one iPhone and one Android phone for real-device testing (plus a cheap Android to test on slow phones), an external monitor, two internet sources and power backup. Most of your Nigerian users are on mid-range Android phones, so test there, not just on your own phone.

Key takeaways

  • Browser device emulation isn't enough. iPhone Safari behaves differently, and you can only test it on a real iPhone.
  • Test on a cheap Android too. That is what most visitors to Nigerian websites use.
  • 16GB RAM is the real minimum for modern dev tools, browsers and Docker.
  • An extra monitor is the single biggest productivity upgrade.

1. The laptop

  • RAM: 16GB minimum; 32GB if you run Docker, Android emulators or large builds.
  • Storage: 512GB SSD. node_modules, Docker images and simulators add up fast.
  • Battery: 8+ hours of real use, because power cuts happen. Apple's M-series MacBooks are hard to beat here.
  • Mac or Windows? You need a Mac to build iOS apps. For web development either works; Windows users should use WSL for a Linux-like setup.

2. Test phones: the part most developers skip

Chrome DevTools' device mode is useful, but it isn't a real phone. Safari on iPhone has its own bugs (100vh, fixed headers, date inputs, video autoplay), touch and keyboard behaviour differ, and real network speeds are slower than your office WiFi. A practical test kit:

3. Monitor, keyboard and mouse

  • A 24"–27" monitor (1440p is great for code) roughly doubles your usable screen space.
  • A comfortable keyboard and mouse save your wrists over long sessions. Mac users like the Magic Mouse (from ₦150,000).
  • A laptop stand puts the screen at eye level and saves your neck.

4. An iPad as a second screen

With a Mac, an iPad works as a wireless second display (Sidecar), which is perfect for docs or a live preview when you work from a café or co-working space. The iPad Air 11" M3 (from ₦899,000) with a keyboard case is also a capable travel machine for SSH and light coding.

5. Internet and power

  • Two internet sources from different providers (fibre or 5G plus a mobile hotspot), so a network outage doesn't stop a deploy.
  • An inverter or power station for the laptop, monitor and router, and a UPS for the router.
  • A good power bank that can charge your laptop over USB-C (65W or more).

6. Calls and focus

Noise-cancelling earbuds make stand-ups and client calls much better in a noisy Lagos environment: AirPods Pro 3 (from ₦330,000) or Galaxy Buds 3 Pro (from ₦220,000).

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an iPhone to test websites?

Yes, for serious work. Safari on iOS uses WebKit and has behaviour you can't fully reproduce on Windows or Android, and depending on your audience, a large share of your paying visitors may be on iPhones.

How much RAM does a web developer need?

16GB is the practical minimum today. Choose 32GB if you run Docker, Android emulators or iOS simulators regularly.

Which cheap phone should I use to test performance?

An entry-level Samsung like the Galaxy A16 or A07 shows you how your site feels on the phones many Nigerian users actually have.
