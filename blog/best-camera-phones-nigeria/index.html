Best Camera Phones to Buy in Nigeria (2026)

By Mikee Gadget Plug • Updated November 26, 2026 • 6 min read

Short answer

For video and content creation, get the iPhone 18 Pro Max. For the best everyday photos and night shots, get the Pixel 11 Pro XL. For zoom and detail, get the Galaxy S26 Ultra. On a budget, a UK used iPhone 15 Pro Max or Pixel 10 Pro still takes excellent pictures.

Key takeaways

  • Choose by what you shoot: video (iPhone), photos (Pixel), zoom (Samsung Ultra).
  • Megapixels are not everything: processing, stabilisation and the telephoto lens matter more.
  • Last year's flagship, UK used, often beats this year's mid-range phone for camera quality.
  • Get at least 256GB if you shoot a lot of 4K video.

1. iPhone 18 Pro Max: best for video and creators

Every rear camera is 48MP, and the main camera has a variable aperture (ƒ/1.48 to ƒ/4.0), so you get more control over background blur and sharpness. The 4x telephoto also gives 8x optical-quality zoom. Where it really pulls ahead is video: 4K Dolby Vision at up to 120fps, very smooth stabilisation, and pro formats like ProRes and Apple Log 2. If you shoot Reels, TikToks, YouTube or events, this is the one.

2. Pixel 11 Pro XL: best for photos

Google's camera processing makes it very hard to take a bad photo. It has a 50MP main camera, a 48MP ultra wide with macro, and a 48MP 5x telephoto with optical-quality zoom at 10x. Night Sight is excellent for Lagos nightlife and indoor events, and skin tones look natural. The 42MP selfie camera is one of the best for group selfies. It's also much cheaper than the iPhone here.

3. Galaxy S26 Ultra: best for zoom

Samsung's Ultra has four rear cameras: a 200MP main camera (now with a brighter ƒ/1.4 lens), a 50MP ultra wide, a 10MP 3x telephoto and a 50MP 5x periscope. That makes it the most flexible camera here for stage events, sports and wildlife. Colours are brighter and punchier than Pixel or iPhone, which many people like for social media.

Best camera phones on a budget

  • Pixel 10 Pro / Pixel 9 Pro: Pixel photo quality for much less. Pixel 9 Pro is from ₦667,000.
  • iPhone 15 Pro Max (UK used): 5x telephoto and great video at a far lower price than new iPhones.
  • Galaxy S23 Ultra / S22 Ultra: still excellent zoom. S22 Ultra is from ₦430,000.
  • Pixel 8a / 9a: the cheapest way into Google's camera processing. Pixel 9a is from ₦417,000.

Tips to get better photos from any phone

  1. Wipe the lens. Most blurry photos are just a dirty lens.
  2. Use the 2x or 3x lens for portraits; faces look more natural than with the main camera up close.
  3. Turn on 4K at 30fps for everyday video; it looks sharp and saves storage compared with 60fps.
  4. For night photos, hold still for two to three seconds after tapping the shutter.
  5. Back up regularly. 4K video fills 128GB very quickly.

Frequently asked questions

Which phone has the best camera in Nigeria right now?

For video, the iPhone 18 Pro Max. For photos, the Pixel 11 Pro XL. For zoom, the Galaxy S26 Ultra. All three are available at Mikee Gadget Plug.

What is the best cheap camera phone?

A UK used Pixel (8a, 9a or 9 Pro) gives the best photo quality for the money. For video on a budget, a UK used iPhone 13 or 14 Pro is a strong choice.

Is a 200MP camera better than 48MP?

Not automatically. More megapixels help with cropping and detail in good light, but processing, sensor size and lenses matter more for most photos.
