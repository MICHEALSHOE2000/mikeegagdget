YOUR UPGRADE, AT A GLANCE

Request an inspection quote ↗

Planning estimate only. This is not a buying offer. Final value depends on inspection, ownership checks, current demand and the unit you choose.

How is the estimate worked out?

We use the catalogue guide price for your exact model and storage, then apply an indicative condition allowance: excellent 65–75%, good 55–65%, fair 40–50%. A weak battery reduces those percentages by 8 points. These are planning assumptions, not approved buy-back prices. Faults, repairs, locks and unknown prices require a manual quote.

SWAP WITH CLARITY

Before you trade.

Is the online value guaranteed?

No. This is an indicative planning range. The final offer is confirmed after inspection and may be outside this range.

What if my phone has a fault?

Choose the relevant issue in the form. We will prepare a manual inspection enquiry instead of displaying an unreliable value.

Can I finance the balance of a swap?

Ask the team whether swap credit and financing can be combined for your exact deal. The online swap and finance estimates are separate and are not an approval to combine them.

What if my phone is worth more than the target?

The calculator will show no estimated top-up where applicable. Any surplus, downgrade exchange or cash difference needs a separate agreement; a payout is not guaranteed.
