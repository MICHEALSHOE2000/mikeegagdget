BEFORE YOU MAKE YOUR MOVE

A few things
worth knowing.

Still have a question? We’re a message away.

Ask us on WhatsApp ↗
Can I swap a Samsung or Google Pixel?

Yes, send your model and condition for an inspection quote. Where no catalogue price is available, the calculator will request a manual valuation instead of inventing a price.

Are the prices final?

Displayed prices are catalogue guides. The team confirms the exact storage, condition, current price and availability before payment.

How does paying in instalments work?

Choose a phone and use the financing calculator to see the deposit, plan cost and repayment schedule. Applications are reviewed; using the calculator does not approve financing.

Can I inspect the phone?

Ask to inspect the exact device at our Computer Village store. For delivery, agree on condition evidence, payment, delivery fees and after-sales terms first.
