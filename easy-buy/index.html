What this estimate includes

Deposit plus repayment of the remaining balance and the flat plan cost. Delivery or any separately quoted fees are not included. Ask for the complete written terms before paying.

The maths, made simple.

Deposit = price × 40%. Plan cost = remaining balance × 20% × months. Instalments repay the balance plus plan cost. This is a flat-cost estimate, not an APR.

Weekly plans use four instalments per planning month; twice-monthly plans use two. Actual due dates and collection terms are agreed before payment.

KNOW BEFORE YOU COMMIT

Your questions,
answered.

What do I need to apply?

Start with your chosen phone and preferred plan. The team will confirm identification and other eligibility requirements directly. Do not send bank PINs, passwords or OTPs.

When do I get the phone?

Collection or delivery depends on the approved arrangement. Confirm the handover point and any verification requirements before paying your deposit.

Can Samsung and Pixel phones be financed?

Ask for a device-specific quote. We do not calculate an automatic plan for models without confirmed finance eligibility or a usable catalogue guide price.

Can I repay early or change my phone while paying?

Ask for the early-settlement and swap-during-repayment terms in writing before accepting your plan. Do not assume the online estimate changes your agreement.

What happens if I miss a payment?

Contact the team promptly. Late fees, collection steps and any device restrictions must be explained in the written agreement before you commit.
