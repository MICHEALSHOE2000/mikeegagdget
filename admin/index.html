Welcome

Here is what needs your attention today.

Your daily routine

  1. Bank transfers: when a customer sends “I’ve sent the money” on WhatsApp, open Orders → Waiting for bank transfer. Check your bank app. If the money is there, tap Money received.
  2. Paid orders: open Orders → Paid – send out. Prepare the phone, then change the delivery step to On the way and later Delivered.
  3. Reservations: open Reservations. Confirm new requests once you know the item is available, and record payment when the customer pays in the store.
  4. Prices: open Prices & products, search for the phone, type the new price and tap Save.

Nothing you do here can take money from anyone. If you are unsure, ask before tapping a green button.

Orders

Newest first. Tap a filter to see only the orders you need.

Reservations

“Reserve Now, Pay at Pickup” requests. Customers pay nothing online. Check the item is available, then confirm.

Steps: 1) Check availability, then tap Confirm (or Reject). The customer is told the pickup deadline. 2) When the phone is ready, tap Ready for pickup. 3) When they pay in the store, tap Payment received at store. Never tap it before the money is in your hand or account.

Delivery prices

Customers see these prices at checkout and pay them with their order. Change a price and tap Save. Leave a price empty to stop delivery to that area (customers can still choose free store pickup).

Lagos home delivery (by LGA)

Other states — transport park pickup

Prices & products

To change a price: search for the phone, type the new price, then tap Save. The new price shows on the website straight away. Orders already placed keep their old price.

Show on website switched off = customers cannot see or buy that option.

Hot Deals & homepage

Choose which phones appear in Hot Deals and at the top of the homepage. Nothing changes on the website until you tap Publish.
