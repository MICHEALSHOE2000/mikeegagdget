7-day warranty

Every product sold by Mikee Gadget Plug includes a 7-day warranty, counted from the day you receive or collect it.

Reporting a fault

If your product is faulty or is not what you ordered, contact us within the warranty period with your order reference and a description of the problem. We will assess it and arrange the next step with you.

Test before you pay

When you buy in store or collect an order at our Computer Village store, you can inspect and test the device before you pay. For delivered orders, payment is made online first; check the device as soon as it arrives.

What the warranty covers

Faults that were present when you received the device. We repair or replace the device and, if neither is possible, refund you in full, as set out in our Refund and Returns Policy.

What it does not cover

Damage after delivery from drops, liquid, misuse or repairs by anyone other than Mikee Gadget Plug, and loss of data. Please back up your device.

Nothing on this page limits any right you have under Nigerian consumer protection law.
