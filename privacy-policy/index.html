Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Mikee Gadget Plug collects, why, who we share it with, how we protect it, and the rights you have under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

1. Who is responsible for your information

Mikee Gadget Plug, 1 Ola Ayeni Street, Off Simbiat Abiola Way, Computer Village, Ikeja, Lagos, is responsible for the personal information described here. Contact us about privacy at support@mikeegadget.com.ng, on WhatsApp or at 0708 686 5133.

2. What we collect

  • Order details you give us: first and last name, phone number, email address, delivery address, city and state, order notes, and what you buy.
  • Payment information: Paystack processes your payment. We receive the payment reference, amount and status, but not your full card or bank details.
  • Pay Small Small: if you choose Credit Direct, Credit Direct collects the information it needs to assess your application, under its own privacy policy. We receive the application and payment status for your order.
  • Messages: what you send us on WhatsApp, by phone or by email, for example about an order or a swap.
  • Information kept on your device: your cart, saved items, filters and which advert brought you to our site are stored in your browser, not on our servers. You can clear them in your browser settings.
  • Advertising measurement: we use the TikTok Pixel. It uses cookies and similar technology to record pages you visit and actions you take (for example, adding to cart), so we can measure our TikTok adverts.

3. How we use it, and our legal basis

  • To process, deliver and support your order, and to provide the warranty: performance of a contract.
  • To keep accounting and tax records and to meet legal obligations: legal obligation.
  • To prevent fraud and keep our website secure: legitimate interests.
  • To measure our adverts and improve our website: legitimate interests or, where required, your consent.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Who we share it with

  • Paystack: to take your payment.
  • Credit Direct: only if you choose Pay Small Small.
  • Delivery partners: your name, phone number and address, so they can deliver your order.
  • Service providers that run our website: secure hosting and database providers that store information for us under contract.
  • TikTok: advertising measurement data, as described above.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it.

5. Where it is stored and how we protect it

Your information is stored with cloud providers whose servers may be outside Nigeria, for example in the European Union. Where information is transferred, we rely on providers that protect it to standards the NDPA recognises.

All connections to our website are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to order information is limited to authorised staff who sign in securely. Payment card data is handled only by Paystack.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them to deliver, support and warrant your order. After that, we keep them for as long as accounting and tax laws require, generally up to 6 years. Then we delete or anonymise them. Information in your browser stays until you clear it.

7. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can:

  • ask for a copy of your personal information;
  • ask us to correct it;
  • ask us to delete it;
  • object to or restrict how we use it;
  • withdraw consent at any time; and
  • ask for your information in a portable format.

Contact us to use any of these rights. We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

8. Cookies and advertising choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also limit personalised adverts in your TikTok settings. Our website still works without advertising cookies.

9. Children

Our website is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the date at the top of this page.

Nothing on this page limits any right you have under Nigerian consumer protection law.
